Carpet water damage: save it or replace it

Carpet water damage restoration in Wheeling, IL comes down to two questions: what kind of water, and how long. Clean water from a supply line that is dried within about a day or two can often be extracted and dried in place. Water from a sewer, a flood or a long leak usually means the carpet pad and sometimes the carpet go. The pad is the sponge, and it holds water long after the surface feels dry.

Lift a corner at the wall to look at the tack strip and the pad. If it smells musty, call right away. Do not walk on soaked carpet more than you must, and keep furniture legs off with foil or blocks so they do not stain.

How a carpet is dried

The crew starts with extraction, using a weighted wand to pull water out of the carpet and pad. Then air movers set at floor level blow across the surface, and a dehumidifier removes moisture from the room. Sometimes carpet is lifted and the pad is removed while the carpet is dried over it, which is called floating the carpet.

Carpet on a concrete slab, such as in a lower level, tends to wick water along the tack strip and into the baseboard. Carpet over wood subfloors also lets water into the wood below. Readings under the carpet are as important as readings on top.

When the pad has to go

Padding is cheap to replace and almost impossible to clean. If it has contacted contaminated water, or if it stays wet past about two days, we recommend replacing it. Replacing pad costs less than dealing with odor and mold later. The decision is explained and written up.

If the soak came from a sewer or flood, see the sewage backup page for the safe approach. Carpet in lower levels is covered on the basement page, and a damp closet near an air handler is discussed under AC leaks.

What to do before the crew arrives

  • Stop the water at its source if you can
  • Remove small items, rugs and furniture from the wet area
  • Place foil or plastic blocks under furniture legs on wet carpet
  • Open windows if the weather allows and run the fan on low
  • Photograph the wet area before anything is moved

Avoid using a household vacuum on wet carpet. Do not turn on the heat to speed things up, since warm damp air near wet pad can encourage odor. Tell us how long the carpet has been wet, since it shapes the plan.

Stairs and hallways are easy to overlook. Water follows the path of least resistance, so check the room below and any closet that shares the wall.

Odor, stains and drying time

Odor is the clearest sign that carpet or pad stayed wet too long. A sour or musty smell after drying usually means moisture remains in the pad or on the slab. We recheck readings before calling the carpet dry, and we say so plainly if replacement is the better path.

What affects carpet restoration cost

Carpet jobs are priced by area and decisions about the pad:

  • Square footage and carpet type
  • Water category and whether pad is replaced
  • Subfloor or slab drying needed
  • Furniture that must be moved
  • Drying days and equipment

Carpet questions

Can wet carpet grow mold?

It can if it stays wet for several days. Fast drying is the best protection.

Will the carpet shrink or stretch?

Sometimes. After drying, carpet may need re-stretching.

Can a carpet cleaner fix wet carpet?

No. A cleaner extracts surface water but does not dry the pad or subfloor.
