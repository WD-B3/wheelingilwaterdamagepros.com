Storm water damage: what to do after the storm

Storm water damage restoration in Wheeling, IL deals with water that entered through the building envelope during a rain, wind or ice event. Wind pushes rain sideways under siding and around windows, and ice dams force melted snow back under shingles. After the storm, stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings, avoid downed power lines outside and call our 24/7 line.

Do not climb on a wet roof. If a branch or debris has opened the roof, a roofer can tarp it, and tarping the same day limits additional water. Move belongings away from wet walls and photograph the damage before cleanup.

How we dry storm damage

A crew traces where the water went, extracts what is standing and dries wet insulation and drywall. Wet insulation is often removed because it holds water. When ceilings are involved, follow the repair path on the ceiling water damage page, and for the lower level read about the basement cleanup.

Documenting storm damage before you clean up

Storm claims depend on clear records. Photograph the damage as you find it, from wide shots to close-ups of the entry point, and keep any debris or damaged materials your insurer may want to see. Record the date and time of the storm and when you first saw water.

Emergency repairs, such as a roof tarp or plywood over a broken window, are normal and worth documenting with receipts. Avoid permanent repairs until your insurer has had a chance to review, unless safety demands it. Ask your insurer what they require before you sign anything for repair work.

Storm questions

Is storm water damage covered?

Wind and hail damage that opens the structure is often covered. Flood damage from rising water usually is not. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Should the roofer or the restoration crew come first?

A roofer should stop the leak. We dry what is inside. Both can be done in the same day.

Can ice dams cause interior damage?

Yes. Melted snow can back up under shingles and drip into walls and ceilings.

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. Storms do not respect business hours. The emergency line is answered nights, weekends and holidays. Move belongings away from wet areas, call and a crew is sent as soon as one is free.
