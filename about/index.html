About Wheeling IL Water Damage Pros

We answer water damage calls around the clock for Wheeling, Illinois and the towns next door. This page explains how the line works and what we do and do not promise.

What we do

Wheeling IL Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service line based at 375 West Dundee Road in Wheeling, IL. When you call, we ask what happened, where the water is and whether it has touched anything electrical. Then we tell you what to shut off and send a licensed and insured restoration crew.

The goal is simple: stop the damage from spreading. That means getting the water out, drying the structure and documenting the loss so you have a clear record for your insurer. We explain scope and price before work starts, and we do not guess at a number before we see the loss.

How the line works

The emergency line is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Calls at night, on weekends and on holidays are answered the same way as weekday calls. We offer same-day response when available, and we do not promise an arrival time, because traffic, weather and a busy day for restoration crews all change the answer.

Where we are

Our address is Wheeling Town Center on West Dundee Road, close to Milwaukee Avenue and the Old Wheeling core. Wheeling sits in Cook County, and the Village maintains the water mains while property owners maintain the private service line. The Village Community Development office can answer permit questions at (847) 459-2620.

From Wheeling we reach the neighboring towns of Prospect Heights, Lincolnshire and Mount Prospect. Each has a page with local water and flood context from the town's own sources.

What we do not claim

We use the plain words licensed and insured and we stand by them, but we do not publish license numbers, insurer names or technician bios on this site, and we do not claim reviews or ratings. We do not promise that an insurance claim will be approved, and we do not name carriers. Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the loss, so ask your insurer.

We also avoid health claims about mold. If you are worried about symptoms, a physician is the right person to ask. What we can do is find the moisture, contain the area and remove affected material to the S520 industry standard.

Standards we point to

Two published industry standards guide most water and mold work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. You can ask any company, including us, whether its crews follow them. A good company will answer without hesitation.

What happens when you call

A person answers and asks four things: the address, what failed, how deep the water is and whether anyone is near wet electrical equipment. Then we explain what to shut off, what to leave alone and what to photograph. If the loss is safe to enter, a crew is dispatched as soon as one is free.

On arrival the crew inspects, takes moisture readings and explains the plan. Scope and price are explained before work starts. If you disagree with any part of the plan, say so, since a good plan fits the building and your priorities.

After drying, you receive written records of the work performed. They help with your insurer, with any later sale of the property and as a plain record of what was done.

If something on the written record looks wrong, tell us. A reading that does not match what you saw, or a room missing from the scope, is worth correcting while the crew is still on site.
