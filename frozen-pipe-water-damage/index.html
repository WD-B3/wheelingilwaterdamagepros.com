Frozen pipe: the damage shows up at the thaw

Frozen pipe water damage in Wheeling, IL usually appears when the temperature rises. Ice inside a pipe expands and can split the metal or push a fitting apart, but the crack stays plugged by ice until it melts. Then water flows from the split. If you hear running water after a cold snap or see a wet spot growing on a wall, treat it as an emergency and call the 24/7 line.

Shut off the main valve first, and leave the faucets open so the line can drain. Do not use an open flame to thaw a pipe. Keep away from any wet outlets, and call your electric utility or 911 if water has reached a panel or you smell burning.

How cold Wheeling gets

The Illinois State Climatologist lists 121.8 days per year with a low at or below 32 degrees Fahrenheit at Chicago O'Hare, based on 1981 to 2010 normals. O'Hare is the nearest long-term station published, so this is a regional figure and not a Wheeling measurement. The practical point is the same: a freeze pattern is routine here, not rare.

Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces and the attic of a townhome carry the most risk. A property left empty over a winter weekend is the classic case, because nobody is there to notice the first drip. Slow drips from a split also keep running quietly behind drywall.

What we do after a frozen pipe thaws

The crew traces the water path, starting at the split and following it through wall cavities, ceilings and floors. Moisture meters and thermal imaging find wet insulation that cannot be seen. Extraction and drying follow, with air movers aimed at walls and dehumidifiers sized to the room.

Ceilings are a frequent casualty when the frozen run was in the attic or upstairs wall. The ceiling page covers stains, sagging and collapse. If the break happened under pressure, the cleanup path matches burst pipe water damage, and drywall repair is covered on the wall and drywall page.

Frozen pipe questions

Is a frozen pipe covered by insurance?

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, but policies differ on freezing and on vacant properties. Ask your insurer before you assume, and document the damage with photographs before cleanup.

Should I thaw the pipe myself?

Only gently, and only after the water is shut off. Never use an open flame. If the pipe has already split, thawing it releases water, so shut off the main first.

Can a pipe freeze and not break?

Yes. A frozen pipe can thaw without splitting. Check fittings and nearby walls for slow drips for a few days afterward.

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. Freeze-and-thaw leaks often start in the middle of the night. Call the emergency line any hour, shut off the main valve and we will send a crew when one is free while you follow the safe steps we give you by phone.
