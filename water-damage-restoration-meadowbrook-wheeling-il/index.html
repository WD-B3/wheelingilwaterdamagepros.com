Meadowbrook, south of Dundee on Wolf Road

Meadowbrook is a single-family neighborhood along Wolf Road, south of Dundee Road in Wheeling. A real estate directory lists homes built from about 1950 to 1969 and shows East and West sections, though we have no official boundary map, so we describe it by its main road instead.

The Cook County hazard plan annex for Wheeling names the Meadowbrook subdivision on Wolf Road among its flood-prone areas. The Village Stormwater Master Plan also records flooding of school property along Wolf Road. Neither source says every Meadowbrook home floods, and neither gives a count of homes affected.

Wheeling storms the plan measured

The Village Stormwater Master Plan leans on two storms that shaped its work. One was September 13 and 14, 2008, used to test its drainage models. The other was April 2013, when about 4.3 inches fell over 24 hours and Buffalo Creek overtopped its banks. The Cook County annex lists the April 2013 disaster declaration as affecting 46 structures in the Village.

These are Village-wide events, not a record of Meadowbrook specifically. They matter because a neighborhood on the Village flood-prone list deserves a calm plan. Know where your main valve is, keep a flashlight handy and photograph a dry house now, so before and after can be compared.

What water damage looks like in an older single-family area

Homes from the 1950s and 1960s may have mixed repairs after decades of ownership, so we open only what the meter says is wet. We do not assume basements, crawl spaces or plumbing materials without checking, and we say so on the scope.

  • Slow supply-line leaks behind a washer or sink, covered under appliance leak water damage
  • Water from heavy rain at windows or the foundation, covered under storm water damage
  • Contaminated backup, handled under sewage backup cleanup with protective steps

Permits and insurance

Re-plumbing and electrical repairs usually involve the Village Community Development office at (847) 459-2620, so ask before work begins. Check your policy about sewer backup or sump pump coverage, since standard homeowners policies often treat those separately. Ask Village Engineering at (847) 459-2620 whether your lot is in a mapped flood hazard area, because we have not checked.

Meadowbrook questions

Is Meadowbrook the same as Meadowbrook East and West?

Real estate listings show East and West sections. We do not have an official boundary, so we serve the area around Wolf Road south of Dundee Road.

Does the Village say Meadowbrook floods?

The Cook County hazard plan annex lists the Meadowbrook subdivision on Wolf Road as a flood-prone area. It does not say how often or how many homes.

Can you dry a wet carpet or do it have to be replaced?

It depends on the water type and how long it stayed wet. Clean water caught early may be dried in place, and contaminated water usually means removal.

Should I turn off the power myself?

Only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water. Otherwise call your electric utility or a licensed electrician.

Do you promise a claim will be paid?

No. Coverage depends on your policy and your insurer. We provide a written scope and photos.
