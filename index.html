What water damage restoration covers in the first 72 hours

Water damage restoration is the full job of stopping a leak or flood, pulling out standing water, drying wet building materials and repairing whatever cannot be saved. In Wheeling, IL that usually starts with a phone call about a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a backed-up floor drain or a basement that took on water overnight.

The first 24 to 72 hours matter most. Drywall, insulation and carpet pad begin to hold moisture within hours, and wet organic material can start supporting mold growth within a few days. The industry reference for this work is ANSI/IICRC S500, and it is fair to ask any company whether its crews work to that standard.

Our emergency line is answered around the clock. Tell us what you see, where the water seems to come from and whether it has touched electrical outlets. We will explain scope and price before work starts, and we offer same-day response when crews are available.

Emergency water damage repair: what to do while you wait

If water is actively spreading, shut off the main water valve first. If the water is touching outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, stay out of the wet area and call your electric utility or 911 before anything else. Turn off power to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel while standing on a dry floor.

Stop the source

Close the main valve or the fixture shut-off. A burst supply line can move several gallons a minute.

Protect people

Keep children and pets out of the wet area. Treat sewage or floodwater as contaminated.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics and cardboard boxes to dry ground. Leave heavy furniture where it stands.

Take photos

Photograph the damage and the source before anything is moved. Your insurer will ask for them.

What water damage repair costs and how insurance fits

Water damage repair is priced by the job, not by a flat rate, because two wet rooms can be completely different problems. We explain scope and price before work starts, and we do not quote a number over the phone for a loss we have not seen.

Five things that set the price

Every estimate for water damage in Wheeling comes down to the same factors:

  • Water category: clean supply water, gray water or contaminated sewage water
  • Affected square footage and how many floors the water reached
  • Materials involved, such as drywall, hardwood, carpet pad or plaster
  • Drying days and the amount of equipment the structure needs
  • Rebuild scope once the structure is dry

The Illinois Department of Insurance notes that standard homeowners policies may cover some sudden water damage, sometimes including sewer backup or sump pump failure if you added that coverage. Gradual leaks and neglect are often excluded, so check your policy or ask your insurer before you assume either way.

Documenting for your insurer

Photograph the source and every affected room before cleanup, keep removed materials until the adjuster has seen them if you are asked to, and write down the date and time you first noticed the water. We give you a written scope of the work performed. We do not promise a claim outcome.

Common questions about water damage in Wheeling

Can someone just come look at the damage before I decide?

Yes. An inspection is how the scope gets set, and scope comes before price. Call the emergency line, describe what you see and we will tell you what we need to check. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and we do not promise an arrival time.

How long does drying take?

Most structural drying runs several days, and the exact number depends on the materials, the water category and how long they stayed wet. Moisture meter readings, not the calendar, decide when drying is finished. The structural drying page explains the equipment and the readings.

Is water damage restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. Restoration removes water and repairs damage after a loss. Waterproofing changes the foundation, drainage or grading so water stays out in future. If your basement keeps taking water, we will say so plainly and suggest you bring in a waterproofing or drainage contractor after the basement cleanup.

Do you help with a large loss and the insurance assessment?

We document the damage, the affected areas and the work performed so your adjuster has a clear record. Coverage decisions belong to your insurer, and we never promise approval of a claim or name a carrier. Ask your insurer what documentation they want before the work starts.

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays.
