Burst pipe: what to do first

A burst pipe is an emergency, and the first job is the main water shut-off. Find the valve where the line enters the house, often near the water meter or water heater, and turn it fully off. Then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line. Call our 24/7 line next so a crew can start the burst pipe water damage cleanup while you document what happened.

If the pipe burst above a ceiling, water will follow wiring and light fixtures. Do not touch a fixture that is dripping. Turn off power to that area only if you can reach the panel on a dry floor, and call your electric utility or 911 when you see sparks or smell burning.

Main valve

Turn it clockwise until it stops. A quarter-turn ball valve closes with a short lever turn.

Drain the line

Open the lowest faucet to relieve pressure and empty the pipe.

Power

Keep away from wet outlets. Shut off the breaker only if the panel is dry and reachable.

Photos

Photograph the failed pipe and the wet areas before cleanup. Keep any broken section for your plumber.

Why pipes burst

Pressurized supply lines fail at fittings, at corroded sections and where something froze earlier in the season. A burst pipe sprays or pours until someone closes the valve, which is why a failure behind a wall often does more harm than a slow drip. Under-sink supply lines, washing machine hoses and old galvanized runs are common failure points.

The Village of Wheeling notes that lead pipes and lead solder can appear in buildings built before 1978. If your plumber replaces a run of older pipe, ask what material was removed. When freezing caused the break, the cause is covered separately on the frozen pipe page.

How we clean up after a burst pipe

The crew locates the water with a moisture meter and thermal camera, because a burst pipe in a wall can wet the cavity above and below the break. Extraction removes standing water, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure. Wet drywall and insulation are cut out when they cannot dry in place.

A pipe under a bathroom or kitchen sink often wets the cabinet floor and the room behind it. The kitchen water damage page covers cabinets, and the wall and drywall page covers what happens to the wall once the water is out. A plumber still has to repair the pipe itself, and we coordinate timing so drying is not delayed.

What affects burst pipe cleanup cost

A short burst caught fast costs less than one that ran overnight. The factors are:

  • How long the pipe ran before it was shut off
  • Water category, which is usually clean for a supply line
  • Number of rooms and floors the water reached
  • Materials soaked, such as drywall, hardwood and carpet pad
  • Drying days and the equipment the structure needs

Burst pipe questions

Does insurance cover a burst pipe?

Sudden and accidental water damage from a burst pipe is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. The Illinois Department of Insurance advises contacting your insurer as soon as possible and photographing the damage. Check your policy, and we will not promise a claim outcome.

Should I call a plumber or a restoration company first?

Shut off the water first, then call both. A plumber repairs the pipe and we remove the water and dry the structure. Starting the dry-out quickly keeps wet materials from turning into a mold problem.

How long until my home is dry?

Several days is common, but the drying time depends on materials and how long they were wet. We confirm dryness with moisture readings, not a calendar date.

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. A burst pipe does not wait for business hours, and our emergency line does not either. Call at night or on a weekend, shut off the main valve if you can reach it, and we will send a crew when one is free and walk you through the safe steps meanwhile.
