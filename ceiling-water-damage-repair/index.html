Ceiling water damage: stay out from under it

Ceiling water damage repair in Wheeling, IL starts with safety. A ceiling that is bulging or dripping holds water in the drywall and insulation above it, and it can drop at once. Move people and furniture out from under the area and call our 24/7 line. If light fixtures are wet or there is a smell of burning, call your electric utility or 911.

Small brown rings on a ceiling can be old, but a stain that grows is active. Place a bucket if water is dripping and pierce a bulge with a small hole only if you can stand clear, so the water drains instead of releasing in one pour. Then call. The cause might be the roof, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an AC line in the attic.

Where the water above the ceiling comes from

Ceiling stains come from several sources. A roof leak travels through shingles, underlayment, decking and insulation before the drywall shows it. A leaking upstairs toilet or tub drips through the subfloor. A split supply line sits in the cavity. And a clogged condensate line can drip from an attic air handler.

A common call is a wet spot below an upstairs closet or bath. The water often travels along a joist for several feet before it drips, so the stain is not directly under the source. We use a moisture meter and thermal camera to trace it back.

Roof leaks and ceiling damage

Roof leaks have no separate page here because the damage lands in the ceiling and insulation. A roof leak needs a roofer to fix the cause, and the interior still needs drying. Wet fiberglass or blown-in insulation can hold water long after the rain stops, and a ceiling that looks dry can hide a saturated cavity.

Wind-driven rain and ice dams in winter are two common ways water gets under shingles. For storm-event damage across the building envelope, see storm water damage cleanup. Ask your roofer to protect the opening with a tarp the same day, because rain before the repair adds to the loss.

How a ceiling gets repaired

The crew removes the affected drywall, usually a few inches beyond the visible stain, and dries the cavity with air movers and dehumidifiers. Insulation that stayed wet is replaced. Once readings confirm dryness, new drywall is hung, taped, textured and primed so painting can match the room.

Water that ran down a wall or into a room below is also dried. If the ceiling water traveled to the floor, the carpet or hardwood floor pages explain what comes next.

What affects ceiling repair cost

Ceiling jobs vary because the damage above is often larger than the stain below:

  • Ceiling size and how much drywall must be removed
  • Wet insulation in the cavity above
  • Whether the source is a roof, plumbing or HVAC leak
  • Texture and paint matching
  • Drying days and equipment

Ceiling questions

Is a ceiling stain an emergency?

A growing, bulging or dripping ceiling is. A dry, old stain usually is not. When in doubt, a moisture meter reading settles it.

Can you repair a ceiling that already collapsed?

Yes. Wet material is removed, the cavity is dried and a new ceiling is installed. Keep clear of the area until a crew has checked it.

Do I need a roofer too?

If the leak came from the roof, yes. A roofer fixes the cause and we handle the water already inside.

Do you handle ceiling leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. Ceiling leaks do not keep office hours. Call the emergency line any time, move out from under the area and shut off the water source if it is a pipe. We will send a crew as soon as one is free and walk you through the safe steps meanwhile.
