Sewage coming up from a floor drain or toilet

If dirty water is rising from a floor drain, a toilet or a tub, you are looking at a sewage backup. Sewage backup cleanup in Wheeling, IL treats this as contaminated Category 3 water. Keep children, pets and everyone else out of the area, do not run more water down drains and call our 24/7 line. Do not use a household vacuum on it.

Sewage backup: what to do first. Stay out of the water, switch off power to the area only if you can reach the panel while standing dry, and call your electric utility or 911 if water is near the panel. Open windows for ventilation if the weather allows. Then call, so contaminated water is handled by a crew in protective gear.

Stay out

Treat the water as contaminated. Avoid contact with skin and do not walk through it.

Stop using drains

Do not flush, run the sink or start the washer. More water makes it worse.

Power

Only shut off the breaker from dry ground. Call the utility or 911 for any shock risk.

Keep records

Photograph the backup and the affected areas before cleanup.

Why sewers back up

A blocked lateral line from your house to the street causes some backups, and heavy rain overloading the sewer system causes others. Roots, grease, wipes and collapsed pipe are common blockages. If neighbors have the same problem, the blockage may be in the public main, and the Village Public Works department needs a call too.

Neighboring Mount Prospect's flood page notes that talking to a plumber about overhead sewers or backup valves is one option for houses with deeper sewer flooding, and that some insurers sell sewer backup and sump pump failure coverage as an add-on. The same advice applies in Wheeling: ask your plumber and your insurer, and check your policy for this endorsement before you need it.

StepWhat it involvesWhy

The standard for this work is ANSI/IICRC S500, and you can ask any company whether its crews follow it. Items you plan to keep, such as hard furniture, are cleaned case by case. Soft goods that were wet with sewage are usually discarded.

Sewage backup questions

Is sewage backup covered by homeowners insurance?

The Illinois Department of Insurance says a standard policy may cover sewer backup or sump pump failure if you have that coverage. Many policies need an add-on, so read yours.

Can I clean it up myself?

Small, clean spills are one thing. Sewage carries contaminants and is best left to a crew with protective gear.

How soon should I call?

Right away. Every hour the water sits it spreads into more material.
