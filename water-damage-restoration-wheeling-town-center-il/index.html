A newer mixed-use block next to the Metra station

Wheeling Town Center is at 351 West Dundee Road, on the former Wickes Furniture site beside the Wheeling Metra Station. A 2017 industry report described a $110 million project with more than 300 rental apartments, about 100,000 square feet of retail and a theater, with the apartments and theater expected to open in fall 2018.

We do not know today's tenant list or unit count, so we describe it as a mixed-use apartment and retail district. The Village plan lists a Town Center concept plan from 2010 and a Station Area plan from 2004, updated in 2019. This part of Wheeling has a long record. a tavern and hotel opened at the Milwaukee Avenue and Dundee Road corner around 1836, and the village incorporated in 1894.

Why multi-story water damage is different

In a mid-rise building, water falls. A failed supply line on one floor can reach units and common areas below it through ceilings, walls and shafts. We trace it from the lowest wet area back to the source and check the units above. Contact the property manager early, because shut-offs and access usually go through them.

Water from outside: Buffalo Creek and the Village

The Village Stormwater Master Plan names Buffalo Creek as the largest source of floodplain structures in Wheeling and describes the Wheeling Drainage Ditch as part of it. It does not say Town Center is affected, and we have not verified Town Center flood zone status. For a specific address, call Village Engineering at (847) 459-2620.

Most Town Center water damage is likely to come from inside the building, such as plumbing, sprinklers, HVAC condensate or appliances. That is our expectation and not a verified rate. Water from inside is covered on pages like burst pipe water damage and AC leak water damage.

Documentation for managers and owners

Property managers often need a written record for owners and insurers. We provide photos, moisture readings and a scope that lists rooms, materials and work performed. Coverage and lease questions are for your insurer and lawyer. Permits for plumbing and electrical work go through the Village Community Development office at (847) 459-2620.
