What heavy rain has done in Prospect Heights

The Metropolitan Water Reclamation District describes a flood control project along Willow Road, near McDonald Creek Tributary A. In its December 2023 release, the district said about 60 homes were completely inaccessible during heavy rains in recent years, and that in some years the road closures ran 12 to 15 days.

The same release says the westernmost part of Prospect Heights flooded in June 2018 after more than four inches of rain fell within two hours. The project as announced would raise Willow Road, Hillcrest Drive and Owen Court and add culverts and compensatory storage.

What this means for a homeowner is practical. If you live in that western edge, a flooded street can delay everyone, including us, so tell us early what you can see from the door. Ask the city engineer's office or the FEMA Flood Map Service Center about your own address, since mapped risk varies lot by lot.

Before we arrive

Stop the source

Close the main valve or the fixture shut-off. If water is rising from outside, do not wade through it.

Power down safely

Turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.

Lift valuables

Move papers, electronics and small furniture up and out. Foil under furniture legs protects wet carpet.

Keep people clear

Keep children and pets out of any water that may be contaminated, especially after a sewer backup.

Call the line

Give us the address, the source and the depth. We tell you what to expect before the crew gets there.

Prospect Heights water and what it means for repairs

The City's 2023 drinking water quality report describes Lake Michigan water produced by the Village of Wilmette, carried through mains owned by the Village of Glenview and supplied through Illinois American Water Company under a purchase agreement. The City Water Department operates the local system and has certified operators on staff.

The same report notes that lead in drinking water mainly comes from service lines and home plumbing, and that the 2023 lead test came in at 4.1 parts per billion at the 90th percentile, below the 15 ppb action level. It advises running a tap for 30 seconds to two minutes after water has sat for hours. If a repair opens old plumbing, ask the plumber what materials they find.

Permits and repairs in the City

Permits come from the City Building Department at City Hall, 8 North Elmhurst Road, and the main number is 847-398-6070. The department's permit page says that re-drywalling requires a permit, that plumbing work and water heater replacement require permits, and that painting, carpeting and tiling do not. The page does not mention water damage repairs by name, so call before work starts.

Drying itself is not construction, but the rebuild after a loss often is. A written scope from us lists what was removed, so the building department and your insurer can see what changed.
