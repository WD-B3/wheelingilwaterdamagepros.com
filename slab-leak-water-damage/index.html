Slab leak signs: what you notice first

Slab leak water damage in Wheeling, IL begins with pipes that run in or under a concrete slab. Signs include water bills that rise with no explanation, a meter that moves when every fixture is off, damp or warm spots on a slab floor and the sound of running water at night. If you see water coming up at a wall base, call the 24/7 line.

To test, shut off every fixture, then watch the water meter. If the dial still moves, a leak exists somewhere between the meter and the house. A slab leak is one possible source, and a plumber with leak-detection equipment confirms it.

How common slabs are in Wheeling homes

Many Wheeling houses have basements, and basements move supply pipes into joists and walls instead of under concrete. Slab leaks do occur in slab-on-grade houses, in additions and garages built on a slab, and in lower-level slab floors where pipes are buried. This page is written for when it does happen, not as a claim that it is typical here. Census housing data does not publish a slab count for the village.

How we handle slab leak water damage

Water from a slab leak often shows up at the edge of a floor, under baseboards or inside lower drywall. The crew extracts standing water and maps moisture across the floor and walls. Floor coverings that trapped water, such as tile adhesive, carpet pad and laminate, are checked first.

Concrete itself dries slowly, so we use dehumidifiers and air movers and track readings over several days. A leak under a finished floor can warp hardwood or lift laminate, and wet lower walls are repaired as described on the wall and drywall page. The plumber repairs or reroutes the pipe, and drying starts alongside that work.

What to tell the plumber and your insurer

Give both the same facts: the date your bill rose, the meter test result, where you felt warmth or dampness and what you did to shut off the water. Photograph wet areas before they are dried, and keep your plumber's written report. An insurer usually wants documentation of the source and the resulting damage.

We provide a written scope of the drying work, which helps document the resulting damage. We do not decide coverage, and we never promise approval. Ask your insurer early whether they require a particular process or a leak-detection report.

What affects slab leak cleanup cost

The leak repair and the water damage cleanup are separate costs. For the cleanup:

  • How long the leak ran before it surfaced
  • Floor covering and square footage affected
  • Whether water reached walls and cabinets
  • Moisture trapped under flooring
  • Drying days and equipment

Slab leak questions

How do I know if it is a slab leak?

A plumber with leak-detection equipment confirms it. A meter that moves with everything off is a strong hint.

Will the concrete need to be broken?

Sometimes. A plumber may reroute pipe through walls instead of breaking the slab. Ask for options.

Can the floor be saved?

It depends on the covering. Tile often survives, while laminate and glued carpet pad often do not.

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. Water coming up through a floor is an emergency at any hour. Close the main valve, call the line, and we will send a crew as soon as one is free. Leak detection and pipe repair are done by a plumber, while we dry the structure.
