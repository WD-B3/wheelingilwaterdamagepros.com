Appliance leak: stop the supply first

Appliance leak water damage in Wheeling, IL starts at a hose, a valve or a drain. If a washing machine hose has split or a dishwasher is overflowing, close the supply valve and unplug the appliance only if the floor around the outlet is dry. If water is running, call the 24/7 line while you do it.

A broken washer valve sprays at full line pressure until someone closes the main, and a refrigerator ice-maker line drips quietly behind the unit for weeks. The first runs for minutes and does obvious damage. The second is hidden, which is why a soft floor behind a refrigerator is a common discovery.

Shut-off

Close the supply valve behind the appliance or the main water valve.

Unplug

Pull the plug only from a dry floor. Otherwise cut the breaker.

Pull it out

Slide the unit forward if you can, to see where the water is coming from.

Photos

Photograph the hose, valve and floor before cleanup.

Hoses age. Rubber washing machine hoses are commonly replaced with braided stainless steel versions, and a plumber can advise on automatic shut-off valves. This page covers the water damage after the failure. For cabinets and the sink area, see kitchen water damage restoration.

How we clean up an appliance leak

The crew starts with a moisture map. A leak at a dishwasher is checked at the cabinet base, the toe kick and the flooring seams. Laundry leaks are traced into the wall and the ceiling below if the laundry room is on an upper floor. Extraction removes standing water and drying equipment goes in.

Flooring that has swelled, such as laminate, is often replaced, while hardwood can sometimes be dried. The hardwood floor page covers cupping, and the water extraction page covers what happens when the water is deep. A water softener or sump pump failure is handled the same way, with extra attention to the basement floor.

Preventing the next appliance leak

Check hoses behind the washer and the refrigerator once a year for bulges, cracks and corroded fittings. Replace rubber hoses that are old, and consider a braided stainless line. Do not leave a washer or dishwasher running while the house is empty, since a failure with nobody home is the costly kind.

A drip pan under the washer and a leak sensor near the dishwasher and water heater cost little and give you an alert before the floor is wet. These are household choices, not restoration services, but they are the cheapest way to avoid calling us.

What affects appliance leak cleanup cost

A short, caught leak costs far less than a hidden one. The factors are:

  • How long the leak ran before discovery
  • Flooring type, such as tile, laminate or hardwood
  • Whether water reached cabinets, walls or a floor below
  • Contents to move or pack out
  • Drying days and equipment

Appliance leak questions

Who pays for a washing machine hose that burst?

Coverage depends on your policy and the cause. Sudden bursts are often covered for the water damage, while worn-out parts and gradual leaks often are not. Ask your insurer.

Should I move the appliance myself?

Only if it is safe to do so and you will not drag it through wet flooring. The crew moves it as part of the job.

How do I know if water got under the floor?

A moisture meter reading shows it. Soft flooring, a musty smell or a rising baseboard are warning signs.

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. A washer valve does not break politely during business hours. The line is answered nights and weekends. Close the supply valve, keep clear of wet outlets and call, and a crew will be sent as soon as one is free.
